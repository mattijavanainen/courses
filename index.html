Explain it without saying it.

A party game for solid state physics. One person explains, everyone else guesses, then the phone moves on.

How to play
  • Each round, one player explains the terms on the cards while everyone else guesses.
  • Do not say the term or any crossed-out word. No gestures, spelling or rhymes.
  • Guessed gives the explainer +1 point. Skip costs 1 point.
  • The round ends when the timer hits zero. The card on screen at that moment does not count.
  • Three correct guesses in a row brings out the unicorns. Five in a row calls in the space lasers.
  • After each round you see a short example explanation for every card that was not guessed.
  • Then pass the device to the next player. Highest score after the last turn wins.
